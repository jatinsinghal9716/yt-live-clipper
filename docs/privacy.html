YT Live Clipper

Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: YT Live Clipper runs on your own computer. We do not run a server, we do not collect your data, and your Google sign-in never leaves your computer. The app talks only to YouTube and Google, directly from your computer, to do what you ask.

What the app is

YT Live Clipper is free, open-source software that you download and run on your own computer. It reads a YouTube live stream's public chat, cuts short clips when chat activity spikes, and, if you choose, posts those clips to your own YouTube channel. The full source code is public on GitHub.

Google user data the app accesses

Only when you click Connect YouTube and agree on Google's sign-in screen, the app receives permission to:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload): to post the clips you choose, or every new clip if you turn on automatic posting.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used only to read your channel's name, so the app can show which channel it is connected to.

How that data is used, stored and shared

  • Your Google sign-in token is saved only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user account (~/.ytclipper/youtube_token.json). It is never sent to the app's developers or to anyone else.
  • Videos, titles, descriptions and tags are sent only to YouTube, directly from your computer, when a clip is posted.
  • The app does not collect analytics, does not use advertising, and does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data. Nobody other than you and Google can see what the app does with your account.
  • YT Live Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other data the app handles

To find moments, the app reads the public chat of the stream you give it and downloads that stream's video to your computer. Clips, captions and chat files are saved in a folder on your computer and are not sent anywhere, except a clip you post to YouTube. Captions are made on your computer with an open-source speech model.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Click Disconnect in the app to delete the saved sign-in from your computer.
  • You can also remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the app's folder and ~/.ytclipper to remove everything it stored.

YouTube

By connecting your channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
